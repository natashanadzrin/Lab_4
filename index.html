Welcome to FSKTM IT Club

The FSKTM IT Club is a student-run community for anyone who loves technology. We bring together students from the Faculty of Computer Science and Information Technology to learn, share ideas and build real projects together.

What We Do

Throughout the semester we organise coding workshops, hackathons, industry talks and friendly competitions. Whether you are a beginner or an experienced programmer, there is always something for you to join and enjoy.
